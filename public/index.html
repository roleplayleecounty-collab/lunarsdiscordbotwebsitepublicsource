❓ FAQ

What is Lyra?

Lyra is a multipurpose Discord bot designed for community management, tickets, economy, utilities and more.

How do I add Lyra?

Click the Invite Lyra button and authorize the bot through Discord.

Why should I login?

Logging in lets Lyra identify you and show the Discord servers you belong to.

Can I invite Lyra to my server?

If you own the server or have the required server management permissions, you'll see an invite button for that server.
